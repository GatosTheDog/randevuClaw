Facts verified while planning (corrections to RESEARCH.md / PATTERNS.md):
- The menu button must lead the owner to the Google AUTH URL; the callback route is what Google redirects to afterwards. The earlier note that the button "links to the OAuth callback route" is wrong.
- Callback business identification must not use `owner_id`/query parameters (RESEARCH.md Example 1 did); the state row is the only source of the businessId.
- The `menu:` callback is already owner-guarded in handleCallbackQuery (`business.ownerTelegramId !== senderTelegramId` -> ignored) and passes the HMAC-verified webhook-scoped `business` into handleMenuCallback; do not re-derive the business from callback data.
- tests/jest.setup.ts sets GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI to http://localhost:3000/oauth/callback, which is why the route path is derived from config instead of hard-coded: whatever pathname is registered with Google is the pathname Express serves.
- `tests/admin-menu.test.ts` uses an automock of ../src/invites/generator, which cannot compile when qrcode/sharp are absent. With a factory mock on that one line the suite passes (17 tests verified while planning).
- Telegram's in-app browser (iOS) is an embedded web view that Google refuses for OAuth (disallowed_useragent); the connect message therefore tells the owner to open the link in Chrome/Safari.
- The existing Telegram `sendTelegramMessage` sends plain text; the auth URL is delivered verbatim.
