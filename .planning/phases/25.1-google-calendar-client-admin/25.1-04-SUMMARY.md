# Phase 25.1 Plan 04: In-bot Google Calendar connect Summary

Owners can now connect Google Calendar from /menu with no CLI: a menu button sends the Google auth URL with a fresh 256-bit, hash-only, single-use, 10-minute state bound to the owner's business; Google redirects to a new GET callback route that exchanges the code, stores the refresh token and confirms in Greek via the business's own bot. The live end-to-end check is deferred (see below).

2. Google Cloud Console -> Library / OAuth consent screen: enable the Google Calendar API, add scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events`, add the owner's Google account under Test users.
3. `fly secrets set GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI=https://randevuclaw.fly.dev/oauth/google/callback` (the route is mounted on the pathname of this value; local `.env.local` may keep the loopback URI).
4. Deploy this plan (and confirm migration 0013 / google_oauth_states exists in the target database; plan 01 handled the schema push).

### The 7 manual steps (once the setup above is done)

1. In Telegram send /menu to the business bot as the owner and tap "Σύνδεση Google Calendar".
2. Open the received link in Chrome/Safari, choose the Google account listed under Test users, accept the calendar.events consent.
3. Expect the Greek "Συνδέθηκε" page and the Telegram message "✅ Το Google Calendar συνδέθηκε!".
4. Confirm in the Neon console that `businesses.google_refresh_token` is non-null for this business (check IS NOT NULL only, never display the value).
5. Re-open the same link: expect the invalid-link page (single use).
6. Wait up to 5 minutes and confirm an already-confirmed FUTURE booking of this business appears in Google Calendar with title "<service> — <client>", the business name and service in the description, and a 30-minute popup reminder.
7. Record whether the consent screen is in Testing status (refresh tokens then expire after 7 days and the owner must reconnect from /menu).
