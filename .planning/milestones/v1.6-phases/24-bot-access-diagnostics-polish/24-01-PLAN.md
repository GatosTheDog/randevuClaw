<objective>
Give the owner and clients one-tap Telegram menu access (BOT-06), and give the owner best-effort technical visibility whenever the bot's generic Greek fallback fires for a client (DIAG-01) — without changing anything the client sees.

Purpose: Owners currently must retype /menu and clients must retype /start on every session; and when the bot silently swallows a client-facing error today, the owner has zero visibility into what failed. Both are small, independent bot-ops polish items closing out the last two open requirements of the v1.6 milestone.
Output: Two new Telegram API wrapper functions (setChatMenuButton, setMyCommands) wired into the existing onboarding activation flow, plus best-effort owner-diagnostic notifications added to the two confirmed client-facing fallback catch blocks.
</objective>
